The Nintendo Switch 2 is an incredible portable console. But let's be honest: flat Joy-Con aren't exactly the most comfortable things to hold for a long gaming session. I've even 3D-printed grips in the past to help, and those have been great. But after spending some time with the **[Belkin Nintendo Switch 2 Grip Charging Case with Power Bank](https://link.amazon/B0i4p3Ux9)**, this feels like another level entirely.

It gives my adult man hands the grip and handling needed to avoid cramped and uncomfortable gameplay sessions. Belkin also added a clever magnetic battery and preserved several features I don't want to give up.

![Belkin Nintendo Switch 2 Grip Charging Case with magnetic power bank attached](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/magnetic-power-bank.jpeg){:width="90%"}

#### The Grips Feel Incredible

The grips are easily my favorite part of the case. They fill out your hands in a way the bare Joy-Con simply can't, making handheld gaming much more comfortable. The shape feels natural, the texture adds just the right amount of grip (Taking notes, Nintendo?), and the whole thing feels reassuringly solid.

I've used 3D-printed grips before, and they absolutely improve the handheld experience. But the fit, finish, and comfort of these Belkin grips feel way more solid than anything I've printed.

![Belkin grips with detached Joy-Con](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/detached-grips.jpeg){:width="90%"}

#### Green Was the Right Choice

I was a little hesitant about getting the green instead of black. Black is the safe choice and would obviously blend right in with the Switch 2. In person though, the green looks *way* better than I expected.

It's a subtle, almost muted green that pairs really well with the blacks of the console and Joy-Con. It gives the whole setup some personality without looking loud or toy-like. I'm really glad I didn't play it safe here.

![Green Belkin Grip Charging Case from the back](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/grip-case-back.jpeg){:width="90%"}

#### A Power Bank That Feels Like MagSafe

The included **10,000mAh power bank** may be the most clever part of the setup. It attaches magnetically to the back of the case with a very MagSafe-like snap. The alignment is effortless, the connection feels secure, and removing it is just as easy.

![Belkin 10,000mAh power bank](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/power-bank.jpeg){:width="80%"}

Belkin built in a short USB-C cable that is exactly the right length. There's no long cord dangling from the back or awkward loop to manage. It stays close to the unit and makes the charging setup look tidy and purpose-built.

![Short USB-C cable connecting the magnetic power bank](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/short-charging-cable.jpeg){:width="90%"}

#### The Stand Still Works

Cases can have a habit of covering up useful hardware features, so I was happy to see that the Switch 2 stand still works while the Belkin case is installed.

That makes tabletop play a legitimate option without taking the case apart or finding somewhere else to put the battery. Just pull out the stand, detach the Joy-Con, and play.

![Nintendo Switch 2 stand in use with the power bank attached](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/stand-and-power-bank.jpeg){:width="90%"}

#### It Still Fits in the Dock

This was the compatibility question I cared about most: would I need to remove the case every time I wanted to dock the Switch 2?

Thankfully, no. The entire unit still fits in the official Nintendo Switch 2 Dock with the case on. It is a more snug fit than docking the bare console, but it still only takes one hand to dock and undock which is a huge win.

![Belkin Grip Charging Case fitting in the Nintendo Switch 2 Dock](/assets/images/belkin-switch-2/dock-fit.jpeg){:width="90%"}

#### Built for Travel

I'm especially excited about the prospects of using this setup for travel. The more comfortable grips make longer handheld sessions much more appealing, while the magnetic 10,000mAh battery adds the kind of extra power that's genuinely useful on a flight or a long road trip.

There is a small amount of added weight to the unit with the case plus battery in use, but nothing that has hindered my ability to enjoy handheld play.

### Final Thoughts

The **[Belkin Nintendo Switch 2 Grip Charging Case with Power Bank](https://link.amazon/B0i4p3Ux9)** gets the important stuff right. The grips feel incredible, the green looks fantastic with the Switch 2, the magnetic battery is wonderfully simple, and the case doesn't get in the way of the stand or dock.

If you play your Switch 2 in handheld mode and want something that makes it more comfortable while also extending its battery life, this is an easy recommendation. I'm already looking forward to throwing it in my bag for the next trip.
