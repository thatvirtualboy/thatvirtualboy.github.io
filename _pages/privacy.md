# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Golden TCG app and the website at goldentcg.app.

## Information we collect

Golden TCG does not collect personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, or contact details. We do not sell or share personal information.

We may collect anonymous, aggregated technical and usage information to improve Golden TCG. This information is not used to identify you.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. If you have questions, contact us at [support@raktech.app](mailto:support@raktech.app).
