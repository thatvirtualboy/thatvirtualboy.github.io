# Skydex Privacy Policy

This policy covers Skydex – Pixel Weather Dex, published by rakTech LLC for Apple devices and Android. Updated November 18, 2026.

## Weather and sightings

Skydex uses the location you select or, with your permission, your device’s location to look up the forecast and determine which Pokémon appear. Forecast requests use coordinates. Weather services include Apple WeatherKit and WeatherAPI.com. You can change location access in your device settings.

A saved sighting can include the Pokémon, the time and the coordinates where you found it. Skydex stores Dex progress on your device. On supported Apple devices, it can also sync through iCloud. When you use account sync, Skydex stores Dex and sighting records associated with your account.

## Accounts and friends

When you sign in, Skydex uses Supabase to authenticate your account and store your profile. Account data can include your sign-in identifier, email address, display name, avatar, friend code, friend relationships and synced Dex progress. Skydex uses Vercel to run its account and weather services. For background updates on supported devices, it can register a device token with its service and deliver updates through Apple or Google.

Accepted friends can see the information you share with them. In Friends, you can choose separately for each friend whether to share Dex entries alone or include discovery locations. You can change that choice or remove a friend.

## Purchases and advertising

Apple or Google processes your store payment. Skydex uses RevenueCat to associate purchase records with an app user ID, restore purchases and unlock paid features.

The free version displays ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region and consent choices, Google’s advertising service may process identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where available, open **Manage Privacy Choices** in Skydex Settings to review your choices. [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Deletion and contact

You can delete your account from Skydex Settings or request deletion by email. The [account deletion guide](/skydex/delete-account) explains which records are removed, what may remain on your device, and how to make a request if you cannot sign in.

For privacy questions or requests, contact [support@raktech.app](mailto:support@raktech.app).
